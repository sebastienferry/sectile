  the ticket. Reuse its queue strip and queue feedback.
- Keep the Send accessible name, send icon, and button appearance in both
  states. A contextual tooltip can explain that a busy submission waits.
- Preserve the separate Interrupt action and the existing Enter shortcut.
- Preserve current availability, attachment, empty draft and queue-limit
