# Clarification #619 - Various improvement based on past work

## Round 1 (2026-09-29)

### Request

The ticket had no description and no comment. The owner stated the scope in
the session: "From Taskativ, we need to do a study comparing roadmap features".

Restated: produce a study that compares the roadmap features of Taskativ,
Sectile's predecessor, with what Sectile offers today, and says which gaps are
worth closing. Out of scope at this stage: porting any feature, and the macro
workflow skills already ported by #423 and #426.

### What was read

- `taskativ/openspec/specs/roadmap-epic-grouping`, `roadmap-remote-projects`,
  `epic-quarter-label`, `epic-template-fields` (requirement headings)
- `taskativ/openspec/changes/taskativ-36`, `-38`, `-52`, `-53`, `-80`, `-82`
  (proposal "Why" sections: split Timeline/Roadmap views, epic labels, condensed
  mode, hidden details panel, origin selection, roadmap-to-backlog link)
- Sizes of `taskativ/web/src/components/RoadmapView.tsx` (4,340 lines) and
  `SprintTimeline.tsx` (986) against Sectile's `web/src/components/RoadmapView.tsx`
  (2,427), `SprintTimelineView.tsx` (2,062), `MacroLabelGroups.tsx`,
  `web/src/lib/roadmap.ts`, `roadmapDisplayMode.ts`, `roadmapProjects.ts`
- `docs/clarifications/426.md` and `docs/adrs/0026-macro-runs-and-macro-worktrees.md`
  (earlier Taskativ ports)

A first keyword pass suggests Sectile already has horizons, label grouping,
remote roadmap projects and a condensed mode, and has no counterpart for the
epic quarter axis or for the epic template field map. This is a hint for the
study, not its result.

### Ambiguities, worst first

1. **What the study produces.** A document only, or a document plus follow-up
   tickets under #619's macro. The two lead to different work at the
   implementation stage.
2. **Perimeter of "roadmap features".** The Roadmap view alone, or the Roadmap
   and Timeline views plus the epic data behind them (sync, labels, quarter,
   template fields).
3. **Direction.** Taskativ-to-Sectile gaps only, or both directions (what
   Sectile does better, and where a port regressed).
4. **Whether the study is the whole of #619.** The title, "Various improvement
   based on past work", reads wider than one study.

### Dependencies

- Read access to the Taskativ checkout on this workstation (granted in the
  session). Taskativ is not a Sectile project repository, so it stays read-only.
- The Sectile repository is public: the study must not carry Equativ project
  keys, internal hosts, or the epic counts quoted in Taskativ's proposals.

### Technical choices settled from the code

- The comparison is feature by feature, each row citing the Taskativ spec or
  change and the Sectile file that covers it, or "missing".
- The study is written in English, like every document in the repository.

### Product questions (recommended option first)

1. **Deliverable.** Recommended: a study document in `docs/`, with a ranked
   list of proposed follow-up tickets; tickets are created only after you
   validate the list.
2. **Perimeter.** Recommended: Roadmap and Timeline views and the epic data
   they display; the macro workflow skills are excluded.
3. **Direction.** Recommended: both directions, with the Taskativ-to-Sectile
   gaps ranked first.
4. **Scope of #619 and its title.** Recommended: the study is the whole
   ticket, and the title becomes "Compare Taskativ and Sectile roadmap
   features".
