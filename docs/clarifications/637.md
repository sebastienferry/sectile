## Round 1 (2026-09-29)

### Restated request and scope

On a board card whose ticket is at the `reviewed` stage, the full-chain button (`>>`, which launches the
`pickup` skill) is hidden and replaced by a shortcut that launches the next step (`handoff`) in autonomous
mode, the same launch the card menu already offers as "Avancer en autonome". Out of scope: the other stages,
the server's own chain entry (`POST /api/tasks/{id}/advance` with `auto: true`), the `pickup` skill itself,
and the per-skill launch grid of the detail modal.

### Files read

- `web/src/components/TaskCard.tsx` — full card row (`>` next step at ~l.1073, `>>` full chain at ~l.1085),
  condensed-card menu (`Chaîne complète` entry at ~l.683), `modeActions` (`Avancer en interactif` /
  `Avancer en autonome`), `handleAdvance`, `getWorkflowAction`.
- `web/src/context/AppContext.tsx` — `advanceTask`: `auto` launches `pickup` forced to `autonomous`;
  otherwise `skillForStage(stage)` with the optional mode override.
- `web/src/lib/workflow.ts` — `skillForStage` (`reviewed` → `handoff`), `getNextStepInfo`.
- `web/src/locales/shell.ts` — `nextStep.reviewed.autoTooltip`: "PR prête : la fusion finale reste manuelle".
- `web/src/components/CopyTaskSkillMenu.tsx`, `web/src/components/TaskDetailModal.tsx` (skills section).
- `web/src/types/index.ts`, `docs/CAPABILITIES.md` §3 — `fullChainStopStage` is `implemented` | `reviewed`.
- Neighbouring tests: `web/tests/condensed-card.browser.mjs`, `web/tests/card-model-menu.browser.mjs`.

### Verified findings

- At `reviewed`, `>>` launches `pickup`, whose stop stage can only be `implemented` or `reviewed`: the chain
  has nothing left to do on any project. Its own tooltip already says the merge stays manual. The button is
  live but useless, which is the defect the ticket describes.
- The launch the ticket asks for already exists: `handleAdvance(false, 'autonomous')` runs `handoff` headless
  with the model chosen on the card. Only its placement on the card row is new.
- On a condensed card both entries already live in the `(...)` menu: `Avancer en autonome` (kept) and
  `Chaîne complète` (the pickup one).
- The server chain entry already refuses a task at or past its stop stage; no server change is needed.

### Ambiguities (worst first)

1. **Which surfaces lose the pickup launch.** The full card's `>>` for sure. The condensed card's
   `Chaîne complète` menu entry is the same launch; the `Copier /pickup-issue` entry of the copy menu (card
   menu and detail modal) is a prompt, not a launch. Different readings touch different components.
2. **Replacement on a condensed card.** Its menu already holds `Avancer en autonome`; adding the shortcut
   there would duplicate it, so the reading is "hide `Chaîne complète` only".
3. **Other stages where the chain has nothing to do.** With `fullChainStopStage = implemented`, a ticket at
   `implemented` has the same useless `>>`. The ticket names only `reviewed`.

### Critical dependencies

None: web-only change, no migration, no server or skill change. A `CHANGELOG.md` line under `[Unreleased]`
is due (user-visible change).

### Technical choices resolved from the code

- Detect the case with the resolved stage already computed on the card (`nextStepInfo.currentStage ===
  'reviewed'`), not with labels or status.
- The shortcut calls `handleAdvance(false, 'autonomous')`, so it honours the model indicator like the other
  card launches, shares the `advancing` spinner/disabled state, and is disabled while a launch is pending.
- Icon: `Bot`, the icon of `Avancer en autonome`, so the two read as the same action. The tooltip is a new
  FR/EN catalog string (FR: "Lancer handoff en autonome : confirmer la fusion et finaliser le ticket"); the
  interface stays in French per `AGENTS.md`.
- Coverage: extend the card browser tests to assert, at `reviewed`, no `>>` and a shortcut that launches
  `handoff` with `mode: 'autonomous'`, and that other stages keep `>>`.

### Open product questions

1. **Surfaces.** Recommended: the full card `>>` is replaced, and the condensed card's `Chaîne complète` menu
   entry is hidden at `reviewed`; the `Copier /pickup-issue` prompt stays as it is.
2. **Condensed card.** Recommended: no extra entry; `Avancer en autonome` already in its menu is the shortcut.
3. **Scope beyond `reviewed`.** Recommended: keep to `reviewed` as the ticket says; the `implemented` +
   stop-stage-`implemented` case can be a follow-up ticket.

## Round 2 - answers from the owner (2026-09-29)

### Answers

1. **Surfaces:** both card shapes, as recommended. The full card's `>>` is replaced by the autonomous
   next-step shortcut; the condensed card's `Chaîne complète` menu entry is hidden. The `Copier /pickup-issue`
   prompt (card menu and detail modal) stays unchanged.
2. **Condensed card:** reuse the existing `Avancer en autonome` menu entry, as recommended; no new entry.
3. **Scope:** **reversed from the Round 1 recommendation.** The swap applies at any stage at or past the
   project's `fullChainStopStage`, not only at `reviewed`.

The owner then confirmed the clarification is satisfactory.

### Settled scope

- The rule is "the full chain has nothing left to do": the card's resolved stage is at or past the project's
  `fullChainStopStage` (missing value = `reviewed`, the documented default).
  - stop stage `reviewed` (default): only `reviewed` cards swap; the shortcut launches `handoff`.
  - stop stage `implemented`: `implemented` and `reviewed` cards swap; at `implemented` the shortcut launches
    `adjust`, at `reviewed` it launches `handoff`.
- Before the stop stage, `>>` and `Chaîne complète` are unchanged.
- `finished` cards keep today's behaviour (no next step, launch controls disabled); nothing new is shown.
- Web-only: no server, skill, or `get_project_context` change. The server chain entry already refuses a task
  at or past the stop stage.

### Reversed assumption

- Round 1 assumed `reviewed` only and proposed a follow-up ticket for `implemented`; that follow-up is no
  longer needed, the stop-stage rule covers it.

### Technical choices (reversible, for the specification)

- One shared predicate in `web/src/lib/workflow.ts` (for example `fullChainHasWork(stage, project)`) compares
  the resolved stage with the stop stage along the workflow order; both card shapes use it, so they cannot
  disagree.
- The shortcut calls `handleAdvance(false, 'autonomous')` (the skill comes from `skillForStage`), keeps the
  model indicator, the `advancing` spinner and the disabled state; icon `Bot`.
- Tooltip: a catalog string with the skill name, FR "Lancer {skill} en autonome" plus the stage's next-step
  description, and its EN counterpart; the French interface text stays French.
- Tests: card browser tests cover both stop stages (swap at and past the stop stage, `>>` kept before it,
  condensed menu without `Chaîne complète` when swapped). `CHANGELOG.md` gets one `Changed` line under
  `[Unreleased]`.
