# Clarification: colorize board cards per epic

## Round 1 — 2026-09-23

The board should offer an option that tints each task card according to the
epic (the parent work item) it belongs to, so that a column can be read at a
glance as a set of epics rather than a list of unrelated tickets. Out of scope:
any change to how parents are imported, filtered or edited, to the workflow
stages, and to the tracker synchronization — this is a presentation option and
nothing else.

### Evidence

Read `web/src/components/TaskCard.tsx`, `web/src/components/BoardView.tsx`,
`web/src/components/BoardGroupingToggle.tsx`, `web/src/components/ListView.tsx`,
`web/src/lib/boardGrouping.ts`, `web/src/lib/boardDisplayMode.ts`,
`web/src/lib/accents.ts`, `web/src/lib/roadmap.ts`, `web/src/types/index.ts`,
`web/src/context/AppContext.tsx`, `internal/db/macros.go`, `AGENTS.md` and the
existing `web/tests/board*.test.mjs` / `condensed-card.browser.mjs` suites.

What the code already establishes:

- An "epic" is carried on the task itself as `parentKey` / `parentTitle` /
  `parentType`; there is no card for it on the board. The condensed card does
  not show the parent at all, the expanded card shows `parentKey` as a
  clickable filter in its first line.
- `TaskCard` is rendered by `BoardView` only. The Backlog (`ListView`) has its
  own row rendering, and shares the grouping toggle but not the card.
- The card root already spends its border and ring on state: running
  (indigo), queued (amber), selected (accent), dragging. A colour applied to
  the card border would collide with those states.
- Board-level display preferences (`boardGrouping`, `boardCardDisplayMode`)
  are per-user and stored in `localStorage`, not on the server.
- A 12-entry palette already exists in `web/src/lib/accents.ts`, used for
  project accents. `MacroMeta` (`internal/db/macros.go`) persists per-epic
  metadata (horizon, description, todos) but carries no colour field.

### Ambiguities, worst first

1. **Where the colour comes from.** A colour derived automatically from the
   epic key and a colour chosen and stored per epic lead to different code: the
   first is a pure front-end function, the second needs a `MacroMeta` field, an
   API and a picker in the UI.
2. **What the colour paints.** A left border, a tinted background and a dot
   next to the key are three different renderings, and only some of them
   survive next to the running/queued/selected states and the condensed card.
3. **Which surfaces are covered.** Board only, or Backlog rows, sprint
   timeline and roadmap as well.
4. **Where the option lives and how it persists.** A per-user toolbar toggle
   like the existing ones, or a project-level setting shared by the team.

### Dependencies

None outside the web front end if the colour is derived. A per-epic stored
colour pulls in `internal/db/macros.go`, the macros API and a migration.
No tracker, third-party or data dependency either way: `parentKey` is already
synchronized and present on the tasks.

### Reversible technical choices, settled here

- Derive the palette from `web/src/lib/accents.ts` rather than introducing new
  hex values, so the colours keep following the theme.
- Tasks without a `parentKey` get no colour at all — they stay exactly as they
  render today.
- The option is off by default; the board looks unchanged until it is enabled.
- The mapping is stable within a session and across reloads for the same set of
  epics, and colours may repeat when the epic count exceeds the palette size.
- Colour is never the only carrier of information: the parent key stays
  readable, so the board remains usable for colour-blind users.
- Pure helper in `web/src/lib/`, covered by a `web/tests/*.test.mjs` node test,
  as `boardGrouping` and `boardDisplayMode` already are.

### Open product questions

1. Automatic colour per epic, or a colour chosen and stored per epic?
2. What does the colour paint on the card?
3. Which surfaces beyond the Board?
4. Per-user preference or project-level setting?

Asked to the owner in session; the answers are recorded in Round 2.

## Round 2 — answers from the owner (2026-09-23)

### Settled choices

1. **Colour source: automatic, derived from the key.** The colour is computed
   from `parentKey` over the palette in `web/src/lib/accents.ts`. No colour is
   stored: `MacroMeta`, the macros API and the database are untouched, and
   every already-imported epic is coloured on first render. Two epics may share
   a colour once the palette size is exceeded; that is accepted.
2. **Rendering: left edge bar plus a dot on the parent key.** A coloured
   vertical bar on the left of the card and a coloured dot in front of
   `parentKey`. Neither the card border nor its background is touched, so
   running (indigo), queued (amber), selected (accent) and dragging keep their
   current meaning, and both the condensed and the expanded card carry it.
3. **Scope: all four surfaces.** Board (both densities), Backlog (`ListView`),
   sprint timeline (`SprintTimelineView`) and Roadmap (`RoadmapView`).
4. **No setting: the colouring is always on.** This reverses the Round 1
   assumption of a per-user toolbar toggle persisted in `localStorage`, and it
   reverses the ticket title as well: there is no option to add, no preference
   to store, no default to choose. `boardGrouping` and `boardCardDisplayMode`
   are left untouched and gain no sibling.

### Consequences of the wider scope

The four surfaces do not render a task the same way, so "left bar plus dot"
does not transpose literally to each of them:

- **Board** (`TaskCard.tsx`): the reference rendering. Bar on the card's left
  edge, dot in front of `parentKey` on the expanded card. The condensed card
  does not show `parentKey` today, so it carries the bar only.
- **Backlog** (`ListView.tsx`): rows already show `parentKey`. Bar on the left
  of the row, dot in front of that key.
- **Sprint timeline** (`SprintTimelineView.tsx`): tasks render as chips and as
  rows depending on `displayMode`. A left bar on a chip is too thin to read, so
  the chip carries the dot in front of its key; the row form carries both.
- **Roadmap** (`RoadmapView.tsx`): tasks are already listed grouped under their
  macro, so a per-row colour would repeat one colour down a whole block. The
  colour belongs on the macro header instead — one bar per macro section —
  which is what makes the Roadmap agree with the other views rather than
  restate what its own grouping already says.

### Still open

The Roadmap treatment above, and the disappearance of the "option" the ticket
title asks for, are recorded as recommendations awaiting the owner's
confirmation. Round 3 records that confirmation.

## Round 3 — answers from the owner (2026-09-23)

### Settled choices

5. **Roadmap: the colour goes on the macro header**, one bar per macro
   section, not on each task row. The Roadmap already groups tasks under their
   macro, so a per-row colour would repeat a single colour down a whole block.
6. **"Always on" is confirmed.** There is no toggle, no stored preference and
   no default to pick. The ticket title still says "add an option"; the
   specification stage restates the scope as a permanent behaviour, and the
   ticket title is adjusted there rather than silently ignored.

### Baseline

- Colour derived from `parentKey` over the `web/src/lib/accents.ts` palette,
  nothing persisted, collisions accepted beyond the palette size.
- Tasks without a `parentKey` render exactly as they do today.
- Colour never carries information on its own: the parent key stays readable
  wherever it is shown today, which keeps the views usable without colour
  perception.
- Board: left edge bar on the card, dot in front of `parentKey` on the
  expanded card, bar only on the condensed card.
- Backlog: left edge bar on the row, dot in front of the key.
- Sprint timeline: dot on the chip form, bar and dot on the row form.
- Roadmap: bar on the macro header.
- Derivation isolated in a `web/src/lib/` helper, covered by a node test under
  `web/tests/`, as `boardGrouping` and `boardDisplayMode` are.
- No backend, schema, API, migration or dependency change.

### Exit condition

Met. The owner confirmed the clarification is satisfactory and asked for the
stage transition. No product question remains open. Stage new -> clarified
applied on branch `feat/388`.
