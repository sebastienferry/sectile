## Round 1 (2026-09-30)

### Request

A roadmap horizon tab can be split into sections by the epic priority or by
the epic quarter (both from #627), each section named after its value with a
count, a "no priority" or "no quarter" section last, every section collapsible
and its folded state remembered. Dragging one epic, or a selection built with
Ctrl or Cmd click and Shift click, onto a section sets that value on each
epic through the same save as the panel; epics already carrying the value are
skipped, and the epics whose save fails are named.

Out of scope: the readiness axis (row 35 of the study, #633, which will add
itself as a third grouping axis), changing the horizon by dragging (the
horizon keeps its own controls), reordering epics by hand inside a section,
per-project axis prefixes and custom fields (#635), a batch write in one
server operation, and any server change.

### Ambiguities, worst first

1. **Which empty sections appear.** Hiding empty sections keeps the view
   honest, but then nobody can drag an epic to a priority or a quarter that no
   epic of the tab has yet, which is the first thing a roadmap review does
   with a fresh quarter. Taskativ ended up keeping the four priorities and
   the used quarters plus the four coming ones. Question 1.
2. **Which tabs can be grouped.** Taskativ groups NOW, NEXT and LATER only.
   Sectile also has an Unclassified tab, and the condensed rows already skip
   the Hidden tab because nothing set aside is prioritized. Question 2.

### Technical choices settled from existing code

- **Axis control.** A toolbar select next to the #627 sort: no grouping
  (default, today's flat list), priority, quarter. It applies to every
  groupable tab at once and is remembered per browser, like the tab.
- **What sections hold.** The filters, the search, the closed toggle and the
  "only issues" filter apply first; sections only hold epics that the flat
  list would show, and the tab counts do not change.
- **Section order.** Priority: P0, P1, P2, P3, then "no priority". Quarter:
  chronological, earliest first, then "no quarter". Each header shows its
  value and its count, the "no value" section included.
- **Order inside a section.** The #627 sort applies inside each section
  (backlog order by default). Under the priority axis a priority sort changes
  nothing and is left as it is.
- **Folding.** Each header folds its section. The folded state is remembered
  per browser, per axis and value (`priority:P1`, `quarter:2026-Q4`,
  `priority:none`), and shared by the tabs. A folded section still accepts a
  drop.
- **Drag.** Available only while an axis is chosen, in full and condensed
  rows alike. A drop sets the section's value on the axis shown, the "no
  value" section clears it. It never changes the horizon, the title, or
  anything else. Dropping on the section the epic comes from does nothing.
- **Selection.** Only while an axis is chosen. Ctrl or Cmd click toggles an
  epic, Shift click extends from the last toggled epic in the displayed order
  (sections in order, folded sections skipped). A plain click opens the panel
  as today and keeps the selection. Dragging a selected epic carries the whole
  selection; dragging an unselected one carries only it and keeps the
  selection. A counter shows the selection size with a control to clear it,
  Escape clears it too, and epics no longer shown (tab change, filter,
  folding) leave it.
- **Writes and report.** The drop saves the epics one at a time through the
  panel's path, skips those already at the target value, never stops on a
  failure, reloads the macros once, clears the selection of the epics that
  moved, and reports once: how many were set, how many skipped, and the keys
  of those that failed. Tracker refusals arrive later in the activities, as
  for any queued write.
- **Strings.** Every new string in French and English. `CHANGELOG.md` gets one
  `Added` line under `[Unreleased]`.

### Product questions

1. **Empty sections as drop targets.** Recommended: under the priority axis,
   P0 to P3 and "no priority" always show, with 0 when empty. Under the
   quarter axis, the quarters the tab's epics use, plus the current quarter and
   the next three, plus "no quarter", always show. Other empty sections never
   show.
2. **Groupable tabs.** Recommended: NOW, NEXT, LATER and Unclassified; the
   Hidden tab stays a flat list without drag, as it already stays out of the
   condensed rows.

## Round 2 - answers from the owner (2026-09-30)

Answered interactively by the owner in the clarification session. Both
round 1 recommendations were accepted; no prior assumption is reversed.

### Settled decisions

1. **Empty sections as drop targets.** Under the priority axis, P0, P1, P2,
   P3 and "no priority" always show, with a count of 0 when empty. Under the
   quarter axis, the sections are the quarters used by the epics of the tab,
   plus the current quarter and the next three, plus "no quarter", which
   always shows. No other empty section shows.
2. **Groupable tabs.** NOW, NEXT, LATER and Unclassified are grouped when an
   axis is chosen. The Hidden tab stays a flat list, without sections, drag or
   multiple selection.

### Consequences settled from those answers

- **Current quarter.** Computed from the browser's local date when the view
  renders (on 2026-09-30: 2026-Q3, then 2026-Q4, 2027-Q1, 2027-Q2). A past
  quarter shows only while an epic of the tab carries it.
- **Quarter section order.** Chronological over the union of used and coming
  quarters, then "no quarter" last.
- **Hidden tab.** The axis control stays in the toolbar and keeps its value:
  it is a reading preference for the other tabs, and returning to NOW shows
  the sections again. On Hidden, a Ctrl, Cmd or Shift click behaves as a plain
  click.
- **Empty folded sections.** An empty section can be folded like any other,
  and still accepts a drop.
